Privacy information.

How this website preview handles your information.

This policy describes the current public review preview of the Las Olas Yacht Management website. It will be updated when direct inquiry delivery or additional services are connected.

Information you enter

The inquiry form processes the details you enter in your browser to create a downloadable text brief. The form does not transmit those details to Las Olas Yacht Management or save them in browser storage. If you choose Copy details, the brief is placed on your device clipboard. Downloaded files remain under your control.

Website hosting

This public review website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as network request information. Anyone with the link can view this preview. See GitHub’s privacy statement for information about the hosting provider.

Cookies and external resources

This website does not add analytics, advertising trackers, or marketing cookies. Fonts and imagery are hosted with the website. The hosting platform may use information necessary to provide access and security.

Images and editorial content

Yacht photographs are illustrative images created for the site design. They do not depict a represented client vessel, completed project, or company fleet. Journal content provides general ownership planning perspectives.

Questions

Business contact information will be provided before public launch. Please avoid submitting sensitive information in this preview.
