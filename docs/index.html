04 / Care that fits your course

Personal by design.
Comprehensive by choice.

Compare programs↗
A considered foundation for time well spent.

Essential Care

A focused starting point for everyday vessel upkeep. Combine essential cleaning and routine checks in a schedule tailored to your yacht, with additional services discussed whenever your plans call for them.

Explore this program↗
  • Basic exterior washdowns, typically every two weeks
  • Interior detailing, typically monthly
  • Scheduled sea strainer attention
  • Monthly systems review and machinery start-up planning
  • Additional care arranged around individual requirements

Every program is tailored. Scope and frequency are agreed for your vessel.

More attention. A more effortless arrival aboard.

Coastal Care

A balanced approach for owners who want a more complete rhythm of care. Detailed cleaning, systems attention and service coordination come together around a proposed fortnightly schedule, adjusted to vessel needs.

Explore this program↗
  • Detailed exterior cleaning and glass care, typically fortnightly
  • Interior refreshes, monthly detailing and laundry coordination
  • Fluid, strainer, seacock and systems check planning
  • Repair and warranty service scheduling
  • Pre-departure preparation and optional yard project oversight

Every program is tailored. Scope and frequency are agreed for your vessel.

The whole yacht, thoughtfully brought into focus.

Complete Management

A comprehensive framework that connects presentation, technical upkeep and ownership logistics. A proposed weekly care rhythm supports your yacht, while service records, project coordination and departure planning keep the wider picture clear.

Explore this program↗
  • Weekly exterior and interior care planning, with finish preservation
  • Routine machinery, fluid, strainer and onboard systems attention
  • Service records, hour logs and maintenance scheduling
  • Shipyard projects, parts, fuel, pump-out and documentation coordination
  • Provisioning, concierge, berth checks and pre-departure preparation

Every program is tailored. Scope and frequency are agreed for your vessel.

Purposeful care for the next offshore day.

Sportfish Care

Care shaped for center consoles and sportfishing yachts, where condition and practical readiness matter together. Build a program around regular cleaning, machinery attention, underwater care and the demands of your fishing calendar.

Explore this program↗
  • Weekly exterior care planning, including cushions, metal and glass
  • Interior refreshes, refrigeration care and departure preparation
  • Machinery checks, systems review and service records
  • Scheduled bottom cleaning and fishing equipment attention
  • Fuel, supplies, maintenance and yard project coordination

Every program is tailored. Scope and frequency are agreed for your vessel.
