A more considered departure checklist

Thoughtful guest preparation starts well before arrival. A shared brief helps bring timing, preferences, provisioning, and the small details of your day together.

Las Olas / Ownership perspectives

Set the shape of the day

Begin with the essentials your management team and captain need for planning: your preferred date, arrival time, guest count, and the kind of day you have in mind. A relaxed lunch and a weekend away call for different preparation. Share your preferences early, while allowing the captain to determine operational arrangements. Keep one current itinerary so guests, crew, and anyone coordinating shore arrangements receive consistent information.

Record preferences once

Build a simple guest brief with the details that make hosting easier. Think about meal preferences, dietary requirements, preferred refreshments, cabin arrangements, and any items you would like available on arrival. Confirm these details with guests instead of relying on last season's notes. Share personal information only with the people who need it for the trip, and keep the brief focused on useful, current requests.

Close the planning loop

Before the visit, request a concise preparation update covering the arrangements you have agreed. Confirm arrival instructions, shore reservations, provisioning requests, and any decisions still awaiting your response. Keep owner and guest planning separate from the captain's operational checks. After your time aboard, note what worked and what you would change. Those observations help the next visit feel more familiar without turning every departure into a new planning exercise.
