Your questions, considered.

Can I arrange deck or hospitality help without a captain?

Yes. An owner-operated yacht may need support with docking tasks, preparation or guest service. Tell us the vessel details, itinerary and assistance required so an appropriate arrangement can be discussed.

What information is needed to arrange a captain?

Useful details include the yacht's make and length, location, dates, itinerary, expected guest count and any particular operating requirements. Availability and the engagement scope are confirmed during planning.
