Yacht Refit & Project Management in Fort Lauderdale

A service period should have a clear direction. Project management connects the work, the people and the decisions, giving yacht owners a considered point of coordination from early planning through the vessel's return to its berth.

A scope shaped around your yacht

Thoughtful care.
Clearly arranged.

Whether the project involves a planned haul-out, repairs or selected upgrades, coordination starts with an agreed scope. We can help organize yard time, contractors, estimates and milestones, then follow progress and communicate decisions as work develops. The aim is straightforward: an informed owner, a coherent schedule and careful attention to the work being performed.

A little more clarity

Your questions, considered.

Can project management be arranged on its own?

Yes. A specific repair, refit or yard period can be discussed as a separate engagement or as part of a wider management plan. The responsibilities and fees are set out in the proposal.

How are changes to the work handled?

The project scope should establish how additional work is reviewed and authorized. When new needs arise, the impact on cost and timing can be presented for the owner's decision before proceeding.
